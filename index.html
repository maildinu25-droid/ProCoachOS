Frequently asked

Built for real coaching staff.

What's the difference between this and the individual app?

The individual app runs entirely on one device. The Academy platform is a shared cloud workspace where multiple coaches, with different roles, all work from the same live data across every device.

How do I get access?

Sign up directly at the Academy platform to start your 14-day free trial — your academy workspace is created immediately, no waiting on a reply.

What roles are available?

Owner, admin, head coach and assistant coach — each with access suited to their responsibility within the academy.

Do all our coaches need to be online at once?

No — changes sync in real time whenever a coach is connected, and everything is there waiting the next time they open the platform.
